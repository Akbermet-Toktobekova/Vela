# Planned Steps vs Real Transfers

A crucial distinction in digital financial tools is the difference between an analytical planning recommendation and an actual bank execution.

When your financial app suggests: *"Set aside €40 for your emergency cushion"* and you tap **Accept**, what happens on your bank account? The answer is: **nothing yet**.

Accepting a recommendation simply records a **planned reservation** within the application. It earmarks that €40 in your mental ledger so you know it is spoken for, preventing other recommendations from claiming that same cash. However, third-party planning applications never initiate real banking transactions or withdraw money from your accounts.

To complete the step in real life, you must log into your banking provider and perform the transfer yourself—for example, transferring €40 from your checking account into your savings account. Once you have executed the real-world transfer, return to the app and mark the action as **Done**. This reconciles the planned reservation into actual saved assets, keeping your plan and your real bank balances perfectly synchronized.
