# Debt Payoff: Avalanche vs Snowball

When managing multiple debts—such as two credit cards and a consumer loan—there are two proven, structured repayment methods: Debt Avalanche and Debt Snowball. Both strategies require paying the required minimum on every debt to prevent penalties, while directing any extra discretionary cash toward one targeted debt at a time.

The **Avalanche Method** targets the debt with the highest APR first (for instance, a credit card at 22% APR before a personal loan at 8% APR). Mathematically, this is the most cost-efficient route: it minimizes the total interest you pay to lenders and clears all debts in the shortest possible time.

The **Snowball Method** targets the debt with the smallest outstanding balance first (for example, a €300 medical bill before a €2,000 credit card), regardless of interest rates. When the smallest balance is eliminated, you gain a quick psychological win and eliminate one monthly minimum payment, rolling that freed-up amount into the next smallest debt.

Neither method is wrong. If minimizing total financial cost is your priority, choose Avalanche. If you need visible, early momentum to stay motivated, choose Snowball.
