# Budgeting with Irregular or Variable Income

Freelancers, gig workers, and people with commission-based or seasonal earnings face a unique challenge: fixed monthly expenses paired with fluctuating monthly revenue. Traditional budgeting advice based on a predictable monthly salary often fails here.

The foundation of managing variable income is calculating your **baseline survival budget**. This is the exact sum needed each month for non-negotiable living essentials: rent, utilities, groceries, debt minimums, and basic transport. For example, if your baseline essentials total €900 per month, that is your critical monthly threshold.

In high-income months (e.g., earning €1,400), resist the temptation to immediately expand your lifestyle or lock all extra cash into long-term savings. Instead, channel the surplus into an income buffer or "holding tank." This buffer sits in your checking or liquid savings account to bridge future low-income months (e.g., earning €600).

Furthermore, when planning actions and commitments, only calculate affordability from cash currently confirmed in your account. Never commit to savings goals or non-essential purchases based on projected or promised future invoices until the funds have actually cleared into your bank account.
