# Building an Emergency Cushion

An emergency cushion is a dedicated pool of cash set aside exclusively for unexpected, unavoidable expenses—such as an urgent medical bill, essential appliance repair, or emergency travel.

Without an emergency reserve, even a modest unexpected expense of €150 or €300 can disrupt your entire monthly budget. It can force you into expensive short-term borrowing, such as high-APR credit cards or overdraft facilities, or create difficult choices about skipping essential bills like rent or utilities.

When starting out, aiming for a small initial cushion of €300 to €500 provides immediate peace of mind. You do not need to fund it all at once; setting aside modest amounts like €20 or €40 from discretionary capacity whenever available builds security steadily over time.

Keep your emergency money liquid and accessible—in a separate standard savings account where it can be transferred within hours if needed, but distinct from your day-to-day spending account so it is not spent accidentally on routine purchases.

Remember: using your cushion for a genuine surprise is not a failure; it is the cushion doing its intended job. Once used, simply resume steady, gradual replenishment when your cash flow permits.
