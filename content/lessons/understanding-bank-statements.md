# How to Read and Reconcile Bank Statements

Modern bank statements and mobile banking feeds provide rich financial data, but understanding the difference between various dates and balances is essential for accurate cash planning.

Key terms to know:
- **Booking Date (Posting Date):** The date the bank formally processed and finalized the transaction on their ledger.
- **Value Date:** The date from which the transaction begins earning or incurring interest.
- **Pending (Authorized) Transactions:** Payments you made that the merchant has authorized, but the funds have not yet left your bank. While your *posted* balance may appear higher, your *available* balance reflects these pending deductions.
- **Running Balance:** The exact cash balance remaining in your account after that specific transaction posted.

When reviewing a statement or importing it into a planning tool, verify that the newest transaction's running balance matches your actual current cash. Pay special attention to transactions categorized as "other" or charges with unfamiliar abbreviations. Clarifying these material unknowns ensures that upcoming scheduled direct debits are not overlooked, giving you confidence that your calculated discretionary capacity is grounded in real facts.

### Quick Check

**Question:** Why might your bank account's available balance be lower than your posted balance?
- [ ] The bank made an irreversible accounting error
- [x] Pending card authorizations have been reserved by merchants but not yet settled
- [ ] Future salary deposits have already been credited early

**Explanation:** Pending authorizations immediately reduce your available spending capacity so that funds are reserved until the merchant finalizes settlement, preventing overdrafts.
