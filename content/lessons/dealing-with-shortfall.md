# Navigating a Cash Shortfall and Seeking Help

A cash shortfall occurs when your confirmed cash balance plus expected immediate income is less than your upcoming mandatory obligations before your plan-through date. Facing a shortfall can feel stressful, but following an objective, structured triage process restores clarity.

When in a shortfall:
1. **Pause all discretionary savings and extra payments immediately.** Every euro of cash must be preserved for basic survival needs: shelter, food, heating, and essential medicine.
2. **Triage your bills by urgency and consequence.** Rent and core utilities have severe consequences if missed, whereas consumer subscriptions or store credit cards carry lower immediate physical risk.
3. **Communicate early with creditors and service providers.** Most utility providers, landlords, and regulated lenders offer temporary hardship arrangements, payment deferrals, or revised installment plans if you notify them *before* a payment is missed.
4. **Explore qualified external assistance.** In almost every jurisdiction, non-profit debt advisory services, municipal welfare grants, and community assistance programs exist to help individuals navigate temporary financial crises without fee or shame.

Never borrow from predatory high-cost lenders to cover a temporary shortfall. Contact an accredited local debt counseling charity or social advisory service early for confidential, free guidance.
